Agent Anomaly Detection is a new, out-of-band oversight layer for the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform that analyzes OpenTelemetry traces and tool calls to catch behavioral risks without adding runtime latency to live requests. It utilizes a multi-tiered detection pipeline—combining lightweight statistical scanning with deep LLM-based reasoning—to identify logical anomalies and policy violations grounded in the OWASP Agentic Top 10.
